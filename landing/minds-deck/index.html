The problem

A Mind has its own on-chain wallet — and spends on its own.

Nobody can prove what an autonomous agent was authorized to spend.

  • No proof of authorization. When an agent pays, there's no verifiable record of what the user actually permitted.
  • One mistake is irreversible. A leaked key or a prompt-injection can drain an on-chain wallet — instantly, permanently.
  • Yesterday's auth doesn't fit. OAuth / ES256 weren't built for long-horizon, multi-hop, autonomous spending — and they aren't post-quantum.

Animoca's own Web4 thesis: agents that "negotiate, collaborate and transact independently on behalf of their human stewards." Transacting safely is the missing primitive.

PQSAFE
02
The solution

PQSafe verifies the authorization before any wallet moves value.

An authorization & audit layer for AI-agent payments. We custody nothing.

Authorize

Every spend is checked against the user's policy (budget, scope, recipient) and dual-signed into a tamper-evident SpendEnvelope.

Verify

Any party re-verifies independently. Tampered amount or recipient → INVALID. Within policy → VALID.

Audit

A tamper-evident receipt is anchored on-chain (Arbitrum) — exportable proof of exactly what was authorized.

Non-custodial by design: funds stay in the user's wallet / on licensed rails. PQSafe never holds, moves, or transfers value — it authorizes and audits.
